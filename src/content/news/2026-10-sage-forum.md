Prof. Chen will speak at the Sage Sociology Research Forum, *Into the Field: Multiple Practices of Sociological Research Methods* (走進研究現場：社會學研究方法的多重實踐), on October 15, 2026, 13:30–14:00, in the Lo Chia-Lun Lecture Hall, 7F, Dah Hsian Seng Library, National Chengchi University. The talk (生命歷程的變奏：從縱貫資料到量化分析) discusses studying the life course with longitudinal data and quantitative analysis. [Event details and registration](https://moltke.nccu.edu.tw/Registration/registration.do?action=conferenceInfo&conferenceID=X27050) (registration closes October 14).
